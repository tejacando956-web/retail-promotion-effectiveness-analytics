Key Insights

  • Only shallow discounts pay: the 10% bucket is the only one with positive ROI (0.084); the best single campaign is Home Refresh Appliances 10% at ROI 0.179.
  • Worst campaign: Accessories Mega Clearance 40% — ROI -0.951, uplift only 7.1% and promo margin -9.83%: it sold below cost and still barely moved volume.
  • Pull-forward: sales dip 18.28% below baseline in the week after campaigns end — part of every uplift number is borrowed future demand.
  • Margin compression: gross margin falls from 45.01% at baseline to 24.11% during promotions, while total incremental gross profit is $-427,082 on $2,126,619 of discounts given away.

Method: each campaign is compared with the 28 clean days (no promo, no dip week) before it, per SKU-store-day. ROI = incremental gross profit / discount cost. Full method, assumptions and code: see README.md and analysis.py in this repo.
